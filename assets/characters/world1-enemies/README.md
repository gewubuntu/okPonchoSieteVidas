# World 1 enemy references

The design of World 1's four enemies and their defeat poof (GDD-ENM-02), as the user approved it (user, 2026-11-04: "Approved", and the
cactus buddy's name, "El Cuate") from an animated design page. Do not edit or overwrite these files.

- `design.html`: the design page as approved: every enemy on a desert stage playing its moves at game speed (60 steps a second), its
  lore and every frame. Open it in a browser; it is self-contained.
- `design-sheet.png`: a still of that page.

The sprites are drawn in code by `scripts/art/enemies1/` (one module per enemy, `kit.mjs` for the shared palette and helpers, `preview.mjs`
for a review sheet beside Poncho) and written by `scripts/art/make-enemies.mjs` to `assets/sprites/enemies/`.

- **Alacrán:** the Durango scorpion (*Centruroides suffusus*), the emblem of Durango. Red-brown, two dark stripes, honey pincers, a tail of
  five beads in a C, a cream stinger, big stern eyes. A busy patroller.
- **Zopilote:** a black vulture (*Coragyps atratus*) of Don Zopilote's court: navy-black, a bare grey-lilac head, a pale hooked beak, pale
  wing-tip patches, and a cholla joint stuck on its back like its king's (why it cannot be stomped).
- **El Cuate (the cactus buddy):** a chubby ribbed column with two raised arms, pale spines, a red claret-cup flower and a sleepy face. He
  dozes, peeks and panics as Poncho comes near.
- **Tlacuache:** the opossum who stole fire and carried it in its tail (Mazatec and Nahua tellings), which is why its tail is bare; grey
  fur, white pointed face, dark ears, a scorch near the tail's tip. It hisses, charges and plays dead.
- **Colours:** only World 1's palette (GDD-ART-06) and the outline shared with Poncho (GDD-ART-05).
- **Tone (GDD-VIS-04):** real animals with true stories, charm first; none of them dressed.
