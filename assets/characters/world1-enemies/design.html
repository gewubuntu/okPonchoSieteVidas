PONCHO: Siete Vidas · Mundo 1, el Desierto

Bestiario del Desierto

World 1's four enemies, drawn in code at Poncho's scale (one drawing pixel is half a game pixel) in the desert's 24 colours and his outline. Every stage plays at game speed, 60 steps a second, with the timings the game uses. Poncho stands in for scale.

Ground:
Design for approval. The animation picks each frame from what the enemy is doing in the game (distance walked, phase and step), so the cycles stay in step with the ground. Nothing about how they play changes.
