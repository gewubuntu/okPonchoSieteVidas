# Poncho victory references

Shared by the user on 2026-11-08 ("Victory pose take the image as reference and the sprite."), as the reference for his victory
pose (GDD-ART-04, Open Question 35). Copies of the user's uploads. Do not edit or overwrite them.

- `victory.jpg`: the first reference, a 100 x 100 pixel drawing (1024 px, 10.24 px a cell), front view: the taco raised high in his right
  paw (the viewer's left), his left paw a fist, the sombrero tilted. No longer read into the game: the strip replaced it.
- `strip.jpg`: the victory frames themselves (user, 2026-11-08: "Just take thie sprite for the victory and adapt it minimal to fit
  our colour palett"), four frames on a 4 px grid: a ready stance with both fists up and no taco, then the taco
  raised higher in each frame. Read cell by cell, only recoloured, into `scripts/art/poncho/victory-pixels.mjs`.
