Shared by the user on 2026-11-08 ("I'll give you several front views of poncho so you can create a top-notch and in-style with the
sideview Nintendo like victory pose."), as the reference for his front view (GDD-ART-02: the sprite references guide the poses the main

**The canonical front view** (what every one of these agrees on):
- **Sombrero:** a wide brim seen a little from below; blue on top, a red underside band with a red-and-blue zigzag of teeth; a tall,
  egg-shaped red crown with a blue band.
- **Ears:** both stand up beside the crown, above the brim; fur on the outside, pink inside.
- **Face:** a wide, rounded, shield-shaped cat face; a cream lower face and muzzle with a cream blaze up between the eyes; tan-grey patches
  around the eyes; big round dark eyes with white glints, usually under furrowed, determined brows; pink blush; a small dark nose; a small
  "w" mouth.
- **Poncho:** a V neck; a blue field with white and red triangles and red dots; red chevron bands of white and blue triangles; a red
  hem band; red fringe at the sides; the gold **PB** badge on his right chest (the viewer's right).
- **Body:** short ginger legs with cream paws; cream paws for hands; the tail curling up to one side with a cream tip.
