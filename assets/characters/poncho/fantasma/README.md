Shared by the user on 2026-11-10 ("Here is the reference picture for poncho in ghost pepper mode") as the reference for how Poncho looks
while the Chile Fantasma runs (GDD-PWR-04). `glow.jpg` is a 1024 x 506 JPEG copy of the upload. Do not edit or overwrite it.

It is a reference for the **effect**, not for his body: he is drawn three-quarter on, and the sprite stays the main pixel drawing
(`../sprite-pixel.jpg`, GDD-ART-02) with its frames. What the strip shows, left to right:

1. **Standing:** a bright golden-yellow outline, about one pixel, round his whole silhouette (sombrero, ears, tail, poncho, feet), outside
   the near-black outline. Small gold sparkles (four-pointed stars and plus shapes) and two small flame flickers float near him. His own
   colours inside are unchanged.
2. **Running:** the same gold outline, with horizontal golden speed streaks trailing behind him and a little gold dust at his back foot.
3. **Jumping:** the gold outline and more sparkles scattered round him.
4. **The warning flash:** the same pose as 1, the outline dimmed to a pale gold and no sparkles or flames (user, 2026-11-10: "use the
   dimmed glow for the warning flash").
