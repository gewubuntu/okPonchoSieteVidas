# Don Zopilote character references

The design of Don Zopilote, World 1's boss (GDD-BOS-02), as the user approved it (user, 2026-11-03: "Ok merge it and implement it!") from a design canvas of key art, lore, a pose sheet, his crown, his tunas and rocks, and his palette. Do not edit or overwrite these images.

- `concept-arena-lap.png`: the key art, 1920 x 1080: him circling the arena 1-4 above Poncho, wings up, dropping a tuna, the rocks on the pillar tops. A real 1-4 screenshot with the design's frames pasted in.
- `concept-arena-stuck.png`: him stuck beak first in the sand beside Poncho after a dive, three pale dots over him.

The look, which the sprite follows (`scripts/art/boss.mjs`, the drawing these images were made with):

- **A turkey vulture** (the zopilote of the Chihuahuan desert), chubby: brown plumage with a cool blue shadow, silvery lilac flight feathers with five finger tips, a fluffy dark ruff, a bare red head with wrinkles, a pale hooked beak, a pale eye under a heavy, grumpy brow.
- **A crown of hammered gold tin** with a tuna as its jewel and three points, one for each hit he has left: a hit knocks one off, and he has none when defeated.
- **Three cholla joints** stuck in his back, green with pale spines: why he cannot be stomped.
- **Poses:** a flap (wings up and down), a climb (tilted up), the telegraph (wings up, beak open, eye wide, talons out; flashing red), the dive (wings tucked, head first), stuck (beak in a sand mound, tail up, three dots), the sweep (wings tucked, talons out, beak open), hurt (crossed eyes, wings down; flashing white), defeated (upside down, no crown; flashing white).
- **His tunas:** red prickly pears with pale glochids and a green cut end, squashed flat when lying. **The rocks:** red sandstone, outlined.
- **Colours:** only World 1's palette (GDD-ART-06) and the outline he shares with Poncho (GDD-ART-05).
- **Tone (GDD-VIS-04):** funny because he is vain, never a caricature: no sombrero, no moustache.
