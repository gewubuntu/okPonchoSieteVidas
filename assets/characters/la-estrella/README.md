# La Estrella character references

The design of La Estrella de Siete Picos, World 2's boss (GDD-BOS-03), as the user approved it (user, 2026-11-09: "I approve the la Estrellas design"). No reference images were given ("Design it, show me"); the face was the user's choice ("Cut-out eyes"). Do not edit or overwrite these images.

- `design-canvas.png`: the design canvas, 1920 x 1080: the key pose, 15 poses at 4x with 1x thumbnails and their reach past her 32 x 32 box, the cones and cracks per hit, the eye states, the tejocote roll, the trolley, cable and rope, the torn stubs, and her palette.
- `concept-arena-hang.png`: key art: a real 2-4 screenshot with her frames pasted at true size, hanging low over the left awning in phase 1 while Poncho rebounds up at her.
- `concept-arena-tear.png`: key art: her phase 3 tear, mid-drop where the middle awning was, Poncho on the left cordel.

The look, which the sprite follows (the drawing these images were made with is to be built into `scripts/art/`):

- **A posada star piñata:** a terracotta clay olla (World 2's tejas colours) with a neck and lip at the top and a cobalt talavera band with glaze dots: her 32 x 32 box and her only solid part.
- **Cut-out eyes:** almond holes cut into the clay, a little slanted, each with a one-pixel yellow "curse light" pupil: neutral, glancing left or right at Poncho, wide in a drop or a yank, squinted (> <) when hurt, X's when defeated. They stay dark through both flashes.
- **Seven crepe-paper cones** behind the pot, each with two zigzag bands: cone 0 (up) rosa, 1 and 6 teal, 2 and 5 yellow, 3 and 4 cobalt. Cones 0, 1 and 6 have glaze-white tips (not stompable, GDD-CMB-01). A torn cone leaves a ragged paper stub.
- **Cracks:** none, a hairline from the neck after the first hit, a second across the band after the second, split with a shard missing when defeated.
- **Streamers:** a three-strand tissue tassel at each cone tip, trailing in a glide, flying up in a drop, streaming down in a bounce, rise or yank, lagging in a sway, hanging when still.
- **On a floor or an awning** her two bottom cones swing to the sides and crumple short, and she squashes 1 px when she lands, so nothing reaches below her box.
- **Defeated:** the pot alone with its seven stubs and X eyes, falling without its rope, then lying on its side.
- **The trolley:** an iron pulley on the cable with turning spokes; her rope (twisted ixtle) ties into her top cone's tip.
- **Her tejocote:** an orange fruit filling its 8 x 8 box, a pale glint at the upper left, a dark calyx turning a quarter each frame over 4 frames.
- **Colours:** 16 of World 2's 24 (GDD-ART-06) and the outline #070509; the telegraph's red flash uses the two flash reds every world's enemies share (GDD-ENM-01).
- **Tone (GDD-VIS-04):** a beautiful posada piñata the curse got into: a little menacing, a little funny, never a caricature.
