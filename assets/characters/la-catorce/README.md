# La Catorce character references

The design of La Catorce, World 3's boss (GDD-BOS-04), as the user approved it (user, 2026-11-22: "Approve"; her cold flame, "Yes, this blue-green"). No reference images were given ("Design it, show me"). Do not edit or overwrite these images.

- `design-canvas.png`: the design canvas: the key pose at 6x with her 64 x 32 box, her lamps per hit, her telegraph, charge, slam, hurt and defeated poses, game-size thumbnails on a pale and a dark ground, her plate and her colours.
- `concept-arena-telegraph.png`: key art: a real 3-4 screenshot (the canyon backdrop, before the shed is drawn) with her frame pasted at true size, telegraphing at the right stop, facing left, her "14" unmirrored.
- `concept-arena-hurt.png`: key art: hurt at the left stop after the first hit, in her white flash.

The look, which the sprite follows (drawn by `scripts/art/catorce.mjs`, the drawing these images were made with):

- **A squat saddle-tank mine engine,** twice as long as tall, everything inside her 64 x 32 game px box, outline included: a tiny cab with an overhanging roof, a round-shouldered tank, a smokebox with a round door, a fat flared funnel stack with a brass band, a running board, a toothed buffer beam at her front.
- **Black iron:** iron dark, gallery deep in shade, steel on the lit edges (World 3's colours, GDD-ART-06).
- **Her plate "14":** lamp amber on her cab, the digits iron. The digits read unmirrored when she faces left (her left-facing frames are drawn so; the game never flips her). Defeated, the plate is gone (it pops off and tumbles).
- **Three brass hit lamps** along her tank, burning the cold flame; the front one goes out first, one with each hit.
- **Her headlamp, her eye:** a brass housing at her front with a slanted iron hood for a frown. Dim at rest, full in her telegraph and charge, a ">" squint when hurt, lamp amber under a level hood when defeated.
- **Three pale studs** on her tank's top: the mark that she cannot be stomped (GDD-CMB-01).
- **Four small wheels** with a coupling rod; each has a counterweight and a lit spoke, so four quarter-turn frames read as spinning.
- **Phase 3:** her firebox open, the cold flame showing through her cab window and her ash pan.
- **Her body on her springs:** it bobs 1 px in the charge, squashes 2 px down and 1 px forward in the slam, and sags when she is defeated; her wheels stay on the rail.
- **The cold flame:** #63E6C4, its deep shade #2E9E96: a 25th colour of World 3, hers alone. It stays lit through the red and the white flash.
- **Tone (GDD-VIS-04):** grumpy and proud, never frightening.
