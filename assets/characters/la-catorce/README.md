# La Catorce character references

La Catorce, World 3's boss (GDD-BOS-04). Do not edit or overwrite these images.

## Her look now: the user's model (2026-11-24)

- `reference-model.jpg`: the user's own model of her (user, 2026-11-24: "I want to change the appearance here is a sprite model"). It is the reference for her look and wins over everything below it.
- `design-sheet-2.png`: the sprite drawn after the model, at 8x and 4x, in her states, with the model beside it.
- `old-vs-new.png`: the sprite it replaced beside the new one, state by state, and both at game size. The user chose "The new one".

How the sprite follows the model (drawn by `scripts/art/catorce.mjs`):

- **Her size is unchanged** (user: "Keep her size (Recommended)"): everything of her, outline included, lies in her 64 x 32 game px box, twice as long as tall. The model is about 1.5 times as long as tall, so she is drawn squatter than it.
- **From the model:** the tall cab under a long overhanging roof, with the firebox's cold glow in its window; the boiler in three banded rings; the brass whistle, the block of three pale studs and the sand dome on its top; the waisted funnel under a wide flared top with a brass band; the round brass lamp on her smokebox; the brass joint between boiler and smokebox; her eye, the big lens in her smokebox front under a slanted hood; three brass hit lamps along her boiler; three wheels with a coupling rod; the cylinder and the slatted cowcatcher.
- **Left out at game size:** the rivets, the door's seam, the second grab rail's detail, the wheel spokes' number (each wheel has a lit spoke and a counterweight, so four quarter-turn frames read as spinning) and the steam over the whistle (her armour's plume stands over her middle stud instead, GDD-BOS-04).
- **Kept from her fight's rules, not in the model:** her plate "14" reads unmirrored when she faces left; the front hit lamp goes out first; her eye is dim at rest, full in her telegraph and charge, a ">" squint when hurt, lamp amber under a level hood when defeated; in phase 3 her firebox door is open and the flame fills her cab window; defeated, her plate is gone and nothing glows.
- **The cold flame:** #63E6C4, its deep shade #2E9E96 (user, 2026-11-22: "Yes, this blue-green"), as in the model's lamps, eye and cab.
- **Tone (GDD-VIS-04):** grumpy and proud, never frightening.
