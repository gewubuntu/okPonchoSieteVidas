# World 1, Desierto: reference

`reference-scene.jpg` is the user's desert scene (2026-10-25). World 1 takes its scenery (blue and purple mountains, clouds, prickly pears, agave, red and orange flowers, grass along the sandy path) under a warmer golden sky (GDD-ART-06). Its HUD and level name are not part of the game's design. Do not edit or overwrite it.

The scene's saguaros are not used: World 1 is the Chihuahuan desert, where they do not grow, so they are replaced by cacti from there, such as cholla, barrel cactus, palm yucca and ocotillo (user, 2026-10-25; GDD-WLD-02, GDD-ART-06).
